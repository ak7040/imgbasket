# ImgBasket SEO research and keyword map

Research date: 20 November 2026. Market: English, Google.com.

This is the working document behind the on-page changes. It records what actually ranks, which
queries are winnable, and which page targets which cluster. Volume tiers are directional estimates
from SERP breadth, autocomplete and competitor targeting, **not** Keyword Planner numbers — confirm
them in Google Search Console (queries you already get impressions for) or Keyword Planner before
committing to a content calendar.

## 1. What is getting reach

### SERP archetypes for this niche

| Archetype | Examples seen | Why it ranks | What to copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact-match landing page from a small host | imagepaste.org/free-image-hosting, imagetourl.cloud/en/free-image-hosting, imageupload.app/en/private-image-hosting, imgland.net/temporary-image-hosting | One URL per query cluster, exact-match H1, 1,300–2,200 words, steps, limits table, FAQ | One page per intent cluster, not one page for everything |
| Self-published "best of" listicle | imglink.cc/blog/best-free-image-hosting-sites-2026, picturetolink.com/blog/best-free-image-hosting-sites, hotimg.com/guides/… | Captures the commercial query, ranks its own tool first, links to competitors | Publish an honest comparison listicle with real limits and a clear method |
| Established host homepage | imgbb.com, postimages.org, imgur.com | Brand + short copy (400–550 words) | Not worth attacking head-on; attack modifiers and alternatives |
| Independent editorial roundup | lifewire.com, techradar.com, petapixel.com, expertphotography.com | Trust, editorial links | Pitch them with verifiable facts and a free test account; these are backlink targets |

Key observation: for **"free image hosting"**, all top results are host-owned pages, and six of eight
rank their own product first. This is a niche where a small site can rank with a focused landing page
plus links — brand authority is not the gatekeeper it is in other verticals.

### What the ranking pages have in common

- Exact-match or near-exact H1: "Free Image Hosting: No Signup, Just a Link".
- The phrase **no signup / no account** in the title or H1.
- A 3–4 step "how it works" block above the fold.
- A limits table: file size, formats, expiry, account, ads, direct links.
- A comparison against Imgur and ImgBB.
- An FAQ with 5–9 questions matching autocomplete phrases.
- Repeated CTA, and internal links to sibling landing pages (temporary, anonymous, private).

### Where ImgBasket can win

1. **Modifier clusters** (`no signup`, `no account`, `direct link`, `private`, `temporary`,
   `link only`) are served by small vendor pages with thin authority. Winnable in weeks, not years.
2. **"No public gallery" / "link-only"** has real demand and almost no exact-match competition.
   This is ImgBasket's actual product, so the copy can be specific instead of generic.
3. **Large files (2 GB) and video** are genuine differentiators versus ImgBB (32 MB, no video) and
   Imgur (50 MB, recompression).
4. **100% free with no paid tier** — every competitor has a Pro plan or ads. Say this plainly and
   repeatedly; it matches the query language ("free", "unlimited", "no sign up").

## 3. Copy rules

Use these words because this is how people search, and because they are true:

- **free image host**, **free image hosting**, **free image sharing site**, **upload an image and get
  a link**.
- **100% free**, **no account**, **no sign-up**, **no ads**, **no paid tier**.
- **no public gallery**, **link-only**, **noindex**, **private**, **unlisted**.
- **direct link**, **BBCode**, **Markdown**, **HTML**, **hotlink**.
- **delete after**, **temporary**, **expires**.

Avoid:

- "gallery", "community", "feed", "explore", "recent uploads" as a description of ImgBasket.
- "unlimited storage" without context; say "no storage quota" and mention the 30 uploads/hour rate
  limit, so the claim stays accurate.
- "private" without the caveat that anyone with the link can view it.

## 4. What was changed in this pass

- Home title/H1/description target **free image host / free image sharing / no sign-up**, and the
  page states what "100% free" means.
- Added **four exact-match pages**: `/free-image-hosting`, `/upload-image-get-link`,
  `/private-image-hosting`, `/temporary-image-hosting`.
- Retuned the titles/descriptions of the alternative and anonymous pages.
- Every visitor upload is now **link-only** (`in_gallery = 0`): `noindex`, out of the sitemap.
  Existing uploads are converted by the `schema-v5` migration.
- Sitemap now lists only the home page, nine content pages and the legal pages.
- `/llms.txt` describes the link-only model.
- Footer and home guide cards now link to all new pages (internal linking).
- Updated the home JSON-LD feature list (100% free, no gallery, link-only, auto-delete).

## 5. Next pages to add (in priority order)

1. **`/best-free-image-hosting-sites`** — the SERP is owned by self-published listicles. Publish an
   honest, dated comparison with a stated method, your own tool included but not automatically first.
   This is the single highest-leverage page left. Include ImgBB, Postimages, Imgur, Catbox, imgbox,
   Freeimage.host and ImgBasket; verify limits on their own pages.
2. **`/image-hosting-for-reddit`** and **`/image-hosting-for-discord`** — split from the forums page;
   different embedding rules and user language.
3. **`/free-image-hosting-unlimited`** — targets "unlimited" qualifiers with the no-quota story.
4. **`/postimages-alternative`** and **`/freeimage-host-alternative`** — same template as the existing
   alternative pages.
5. **`/image-hosting-for-ebay`** (or marketplace listings) — high commercial intent, direct-link need.

Keep each page 1,000+ words with a unique angle. Do not create near-duplicate doorway pages; Google
treats those as thin content and they dilute the whole site.

## 6. Off-page plan (what actually moves rankings)

On-page fixes discovery and targeting. They do not create authority. In rough priority:

1. **Search Console**: verify, submit the sitemap, request indexing for `/` and all nine content pages.
   Check the Queries report weekly for impressions on the clusters above.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import from Search Console, then run `scripts/indexnow.sh` after each
   deploy. Bing feeds Copilot citations.
3. **Directories and listings**: AlternativeTo (as an Imgur/ImgBB alternative), Product Hunt,
   SaaSHub, Slant, and any "free image hosting" GitHub awesome-lists that accept PRs.
4. **Independent roundups**: pitch Lifewire, TechRadar, PetaPixel and ExpertPhotography with a
   short fact sheet (2 GB, video, no ads, no account, no gallery). Offer a test link. Do not spam;
   one specific, factual email per outlet.
5. **Communities**: answer real questions on Reddit (r/webdev, r/forhire, r/selfhosted) and Quora
   where a link genuinely helps. Read each community's self-promotion rules first.
6. **Linkable asset**: a quarterly "free image host limits" table (file size, formats, expiry, ads,
   account, gallery) that roundups can cite. Update it when competitors change, and date it.

## 7. Measurement

- GSC: impressions and average position per cluster, indexed page count, sitemap status.
- Target: first impressions for cluster D/E/F within 2–4 weeks of indexing; page-1 positions for
  long-tail modifiers in 2–3 months; head terms ("free image hosting") are a 6–12 month project.
- Watch for: pages indexed but not ranking (authority problem), impressions but low CTR (title
  problem), ranking drops after copy changes (cannibalisation — consolidate).
