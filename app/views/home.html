Free image hosting, and here is exactly what “free” means

{{SITE_NAME}} is a free image host and free image sharing site for people who want a link, not another account. There is no sign-up, no email to verify, no credit card, no trial, no paid upgrade and no storage quota. Upload as many images and videos as you need, each up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}}. Frequently asked questions

Is {{SITE_NAME}} really 100% free?

Yes. Uploading, hosting, sharing and viewing images and videos is free. There is no account, no credit card, no free trial, no paid upgrade and no storage quota. There is also no advertising on the public site. Rate limits exist to stop abuse: 30 uploads per hour from one IP address.

Do my uploads appear in a public gallery?

No. {{SITE_NAME}} has no public gallery or recent-uploads feed. Every upload is link-only: it gets its own page and direct link, the page is marked noindex, and it is not added to the sitemap. Only people with the link can open it. See private image hosting for the details.

How many files can I upload?

There is no storage quota and no limit on the number of files. To stop abuse, uploads are rate-limited to 30 files per hour from one IP address. If you hit the limit, try again later.

How large can a file be?

Up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} per file, which covers high-resolution photos, screenshots, animated GIFs, design exports and short videos. The original file is served — we don't recompress it for the direct link.

Can I hotlink images and videos on my website?

Yes. Upload the file, copy the direct link, and use it in an <img> or <video> tag, a CSS background or anywhere else that accepts a media URL. Forums and message boards usually want the BBCode tab instead.

How do I upload an image and get a link?

Drop the file on the upload box, paste it with Ctrl + V, or choose it from your device. When it finishes, copy the page link or the direct link. The upload image and get a link guide walks through it.

How do I share an image or video?

Upload it, then copy one of the links shown under it. The page link opens the file on {{SITE_NAME}}; the direct link points at the image or video file itself.

How do I post an image or video in a forum or on Reddit?

After uploading, pick the BBCode tab for most forums, or Markdown for Reddit, GitHub and Discord, then copy and paste it into your post. Videos also get a ready-made HTML <video> tag.

Which file types can I upload?

JPG, PNG, GIF (animated too), WebP, AVIF and BMP images, plus MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V and OGV videos, up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} each.

Is {{SITE_NAME}} an Imgur or ImgBB alternative?

Yes. It does the same job — upload an image, get a shareable link — with a much larger per-image limit, no account and no public gallery. See the Imgur alternative and ImgBB alternative pages for a side-by-side comparison, or the free image hosting guide.

How long are files kept?

You choose when you upload: pick Delete after in the upload box to remove a file automatically after 1 hour, 1 day, 7, 30 or 90 days, or leave it on Keep forever. Otherwise {{SITE_NAME}} is a free service, so we can't guarantee that a file stays online forever. Keep your own copy of anything important. See the Terms of Use for details.
