Frequently asked questions

Is {{SITE_NAME}} free?

Yes. Uploading, sharing and viewing images is free, and you don't need an account. There is no storage quota and no charge for hosting or bandwidth.

How many images can I upload?

There is no storage quota and no limit on the number of images. To stop abuse, uploads are rate-limited to 30 images per hour from one IP address. If you hit the limit, try again later.

How large can an image be?

Up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} per image, which covers high-resolution photos, screenshots, animated GIFs and design exports. The original file is served — we don't recompress it for the direct link.

Can I hotlink images on my website?

Yes. Upload the image, copy the direct link, and use it in an <img> tag, a CSS background or anywhere else that accepts an image URL. Forums and message boards usually want the BBCode tab instead.

How do I share an image?

Upload it, then copy one of the links shown under it. The page link opens the image on {{SITE_NAME}}; the direct link points at the image file itself.

How do I post an image in a forum or on Reddit?

After uploading, pick the BBCode tab for most forums, or Markdown for Reddit, GitHub and Discord, then copy and paste it into your post.

Which file types can I upload?

JPG, PNG, GIF (animated too), WebP, AVIF and BMP images, up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} each.

Is {{SITE_NAME}} an Imgur or ImgBB alternative?

Yes. It does the same job — upload an image, get a shareable link — with a much larger per-image limit and no account. See the Imgur alternative and ImgBB alternative pages for a side-by-side comparison.

How long are images kept?

{{SITE_NAME}} is a free service, so we can't guarantee that an image stays online forever. Keep your own copy of anything important. See the Terms of Use for details.
