What anonymous means here

Anonymous means there is no account, profile or username attached to the upload. It does not mean the site is untraceable, and we don't pretend otherwise. Here is exactly what happens:

  • No account or email. You can upload and get links without identifying yourself.
  • No third-party trackers on shared files. No advertising scripts anywhere, and the file pages you share load no analytics and set no cookies.
  • IP address stored. Each upload records the connection's IP address so we can apply rate limits and handle abuse reports. See the Privacy Policy.
  • No public gallery. Uploads are link-only, stay out of the gallery and the sitemap, and carry noindex, so search engines won't list them.
  • No expiry date. We don't delete images on a timer, though a free service can't promise permanence.

How to upload without signing up

  1. Open the home page. The uploader is the big drop area at the top.
  2. Drop the file, paste it with Ctrl + V, or choose it from your device. Files up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} are accepted. The upload is link-only automatically.
  3. Optionally set Delete after to 1 hour, 1 day, 7, 30 or 90 days.
  4. Copy the page link or the direct link from the result. That's it — no confirmation email, no account to create.

Responsible use

Anonymity is for convenience, not for getting away with something. Upload only images you own or have permission to share. Illegal content, harassment, private information about other people and copyright infringement are not allowed and are removed when reported. The Terms of Use list the rules in full, and every image page has a Report link.

Common questions

Do you use cookies?

Shared file pages set no cookies. The main site uses Google Analytics, which sets _ga cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful; there are no advertising scripts, and the upload choice is not stored in your browser. Only the site administrator has a sign-in cookie.

Can I upload from my phone?

Yes. The uploader works in mobile browsers, and you can pick an image from the photo library. The result page also offers a QR code for the direct link.

Do you log which IP uploaded an image?

Yes. It is used for rate limiting and for abuse reports, as described in the Privacy Policy.
