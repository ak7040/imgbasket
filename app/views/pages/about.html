About {{SITE_NAME}}

{{SITE_NAME}} is a small, independent free image and video hosting service. It exists to do one thing well: take an image or video, give you a stable link, and not demand an account in return.

What the site does

Anyone can upload an image or video from the home page. Each upload produces a page on {{SITE_NAME}} and a direct link to the file, plus Markdown, HTML and BBCode for forums and blogs. Uploads are link-only: they are not listed in a gallery or the sitemap, and their pages carry noindex, so only people with the link can find them.

Files are accepted up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}} each: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF and BMP images, plus MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V and OGV videos. There is no storage quota and no account system on the public site. Uploads are rate-limited to 30 per hour from one IP address to keep automated abuse down.

How it works

The site runs on PHP with a MySQL database. Uploaded files are stored in Telegram — the messaging platform — either in the operator's Saved Messages or in a private channel managed by a bot. Telegram is used as the storage layer; files are served back through {{SITE_NAME}} with the same links either way. Nothing is kept on this server's disk: each view streams the file from Telegram.

Two preview sizes are generated for each image: a JPEG for chat-app link previews and a WebP for the uploader's result tiles. Videos get a poster frame in the same two sizes when FFmpeg is available on the server. The direct link always points at the original file, not the preview.

Who runs it

{{SITE_NAME}} is run by an independent operator, not a large company. There is no advertising network, no venture funding and no data broker behind it. The service is free, and the costs are covered by the people running it.

That also sets expectations: this is a small service, not a permanent archive. We work to keep it online and to remove abusive content quickly, but we can't offer the uptime or permanence guarantees of a paid storage provider. Keep your own copies of anything you can't afford to lose.

Privacy and moderation
