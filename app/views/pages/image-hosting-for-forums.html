Image hosting for forums, Reddit and blogs

Most forums don't let you paste an image straight into a post. They expect a BBCode tag pointing at a hosted file, or a direct URL. {{SITE_NAME}} uploads your image and hands you the exact code for each place you post, so the first paste works.

Step by step

  1. Open the uploader and add your image — drag and drop, choose a file, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl + V.
  2. Wait for “Published”. The result shows tabs for each link format.
  3. Pick BBCode for a forum, Markdown for Reddit, or HTML for a blog, then press Copy.
  4. Paste it into your post. Use the forum's preview button before submitting if it has one.

Keeping images visible on a forum

  • Use the direct link, not the page link. A forum loads the image itself from the direct URL; the page link only opens the viewer.
  • Keep the file under the forum's limit. Some boards resize or reject images above a few hundred kilobytes even when the host accepts them. {{SITE_NAME}} takes up to {{MAX_UPLOAD}}, but the board's own rules still apply.
  • Use a sensible file name. It becomes the image title and the alt text, which helps the post and image search. “brake-pad-wear.jpg” beats “IMG_2043.jpg”.
  • Don't hotlink images you don't own. Hosting your own copy is fine; reposting someone else's copyrighted image is not.

What about signatures and avatars?

Signatures and avatars usually accept a direct image URL and often have a strict pixel or file-size cap. Upload the image, copy the direct link, and paste it into the signature or avatar field. If the board rejects it, reduce the dimensions first — the host will accept the smaller file just as well.

Common questions

Do I need an account on the image host?

No. Upload and copy the link; there is nothing to sign up for. That also means there is no library to browse later — keep the links you want to reuse.

Will the image stay in my forum post?

As long as the upload stays available. We don't expire images on a timer, but a free service can't guarantee permanence. For a tutorial or a post you expect to last for years, keep a local copy.

Can I post the same image on several forums?

Yes. One upload gives one URL, and you can paste it into as many posts as you like.
