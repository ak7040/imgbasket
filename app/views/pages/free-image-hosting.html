Why choose a free image host without a gallery?

A public gallery changes what an image host is. It turns uploads into content, adds moderation pressure, and makes every file discoverable by anyone. {{SITE_NAME}} is built the other way around: it is a private, link-only image host by default. Upload pages are excluded from search results and the sitemap, and there is no page that lists recent uploads.

That does not make a link secret. Anyone you send it to can open it, and they can forward it. Treat a link like a password, and use the delete-after timer for anything you only need briefly. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored (an IP address for rate limiting and abuse reports) and what is not (no account, no email, no ad profile).

Common questions

Is this really free, or is there a hidden paid plan?

Really free. There is no paid plan to sell and no feature held back for subscribers. Every link type, video support, the delete-after timer and unlimited uploads are part of the free service. Hosting is paid for by the site owner, not by visitors.

Do I need an account to upload?

No. The uploader works immediately in a browser, including on a phone. There is no sign-up, no email and no password to remember.

Will my image appear in Google Images?

No. Upload pages carry noindex, nofollow and are left out of the sitemap. The file is reachable only through the link you share. See anonymous image hosting for what “anonymous” does and does not cover.

How long do links last?

On Keep forever, there is no automatic expiry — but a free service cannot promise permanence, so keep your own copy of anything important. With Delete after, the link stops working at the time you chose. The temporary image hosting page explains the options.

Can I upload videos as well as images?

Yes. MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V and OGV videos are hosted the same way, with a direct link and an HTML <video> embed. Videos play in the browser on the file's page.
